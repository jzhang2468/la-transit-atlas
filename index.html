Data & methods

Rent and burden

Gross rent includes rent and tenant-paid utilities. A household is rent burdened when gross rent is at least 30% of household income. The tract rate excludes households for which the Census Bureau could not compute that share. Both axes split at the median among county tracts with both measures.

ACS B25064 · ACS B25070

Proximity to Metro rail

The atlas marks a tract as near rail when at least half its area falls within 800 m of a Metro rail station. The rings measure straight-line distance and the calculation dissolves overlaps. The comparison is descriptive. Station geometry comes from a later snapshot than the housing estimates.

Home to workplace

Each selected tract shows up to 12 LA County workplace tracts ranked by the number of primary jobs held by its residents. Schematic arcs connect representative tract points. Moving dots indicate direction. These records cover workers across all tenures and contain neither travel mode nor route. The in-state totals exclude out-of-state workplaces.
