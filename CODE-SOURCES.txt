Assignment Four | Code sources and what's in the ZIP

Why I made this map

My earlier QGIS map looked at where housing applications were filed
near rail in Los Angeles. For this assignment, I wanted to ask what
living near transit looks like for renters. The feedback on my proposal
gave me two places to start. First, compare rent and rent burden. Then,
look at where people in those areas work.

Hyde Park and Palms made the first question concrete for me. In the
two tracts I chose, the estimated median rents are $1,866 and $1,881
a month. The estimated shares of rent-burdened households are 83.0%
and 42.1%. That contrast is why I put rent and burden side by side
in a four-cell legend.

Where the code and design came from

I used the course's Astro/MapLibre template as a reference for putting
several layers and views on one map. I used OpenAI Codex to write the
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for this version, prepare the data, and check
the interactions in a browser. The site uses plain HTML, CSS, and
JavaScript. The GeoJSON polygon and line examples informed how the map
draws the tracts and connections. The MapLibre hover example informed
how a tract highlights when you move the cursor over it.

What the map measures

Rent. The Census Bureau's 2019–2023 ACS tables B25064 and B25070
provide estimated median gross rent and the share of renter households
spending at least 30% of their income on gross rent. The burden rate
uses households for which that share could be calculated. The four
categories split at $1,884 and 58.0065%, the medians among the 2,426
tracts with both estimates. Values at the cutoff go in the higher
group. Tract details show the rent estimate's margin of error where
available. The ACS upper-end code 3501 is displayed as $3,500+.
https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2023.B25064?g=050XX00US06037
https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2023.B25070?g=050XX00US06037

Rail. The rings are 800-meter straight-line buffers around stations
in the November 13, 2026 LA Metro rail feed. A tract counts as near
rail if the combined buffers cover at least half its TIGER/Line tract
area. The rail snapshot is newer than the rent estimates. The buffers
measure proximity, so actual walking distances can differ.
https://developer.metro.net/gtfs-schedule-data/
https://gitlab.com/LACMTA/gtfs_rail/-/raw/a939fcc8ac7a2b6c26bf6962b91e8b0429649c88/gtfs_rail.zip
https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2023/TRACT/tl_2023_06_tract.zip

Work. The 2024 Census LODES California file was grouped by home and
workplace tract. For each home tract, the map shows up to 12 workplace
tracts in LA County, ranked by primary jobs. The records include
workers across housing tenures. The moving dots show the direction
from home to workplace. The arcs join representative tract points,
and the data gives no travel route or mode.
https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/lodes/LODES8/ca/od/ca_od_main_JT01_2024.csv.gz
https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/lodes/LODES8/LODESTechDoc8.5.pdf
https://onthemap.ces.census.gov/

Basemap. Census cartographic boundaries provide the outlines.
Roads, parks, water, and place labels come from OpenStreetMap via
OpenFreeMap and OpenMapTiles. The OpenStreetMap-derived data is

What's in the ZIP

Unzip the folder and open index.html in a current desktop browser
with WebGL. Keep the files and folders together so the atlas can
open offline. If you'd rather run it through a local server, run
python3 -m http.server 8000 from the extracted folder and open
http://127.0.0.1:8000/.

index.html holds the page. atlas.css is the visual style. atlas.js
creates the MapLibre map and connects the chapter buttons, legend,
and tract selections to the map layers. data/ has the prepared map
layers and the bundle used for offline opening. vendor/ and fonts/
include MapLibre GL JS 5.6.1, Inter Variable, and their licenses.

The reproduce/ folder has the scripts used to build the map data.
The Python scripts list their inputs and dependencies. If the data
changes, run node reproduce/build_standalone.cjs to update the
offline bundle.

Final atlas addition | Evening bus service in Van Nuys and Reseda

The fourth chapter pairs census tracts 1283.03 and 1325.02 from the
original high-burden, lower-rail-proximity group. Its selection uses
the 2023 rail map supplied with the project. Rent burden and vehicle
availability come from different 2019–2023 ACS tables. The bus
timetable covers two named stops, one line and one direction per stop,
on October 30, 2026 from 7 to 9 p.m. Pacific. The chapter's Data &
methods section links to the Census, city and archived Metro sources.

neighborhoods.html, neighborhoods.css and neighborhoods.js draw the
paired maps and interactive departure strips using native browser
canvas and HTML. data/neighborhoods.json holds the derived data.
reproduce/read_map1_classes.py checks the original map colors against
tract geometry. reproduce/prepare_neighborhoods.py checks the two
selected tracts and builds their streets, route shapes and schedules.
The README-style instructions at the top of both scripts identify
their inputs. The PDF is evening-buses.pdf. Its overview includes
OpenStreetMap-derived parks and water from the atlas basemap.

OpenAI Codex assisted with GIS processing, interface code, print
layout and testing. The print and web cartography follows the
supplied Mapping TOC Housing Applications Near Rail Transit plate.

Final atlas addition | Rent burden and renter vehicle availability

Chapter 05 compares two 2019–2023 ACS five-year tract estimates for
the same 1,108 study tracts as the original City of Los Angeles map.
B25070 gives the share of cash-rent households with a computable
rent-to-income ratio that spend at least 30% on gross rent. B25044
gives the share of all renter households with zero vehicles available.
The upper quartile of valid tract estimates defines "high" separately
for each indicator. The two tables measure separate aggregates.

https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs5/groups/B25070.html
https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs5/groups/B25044.html

renter-vehicles.html, renter-vehicles.css and renter-vehicles.js
provide the interactive map. data/renter-vehicles.json contains
the derived tract data, and renter-vehicles.pdf is the print plate.
reproduce/prepare_renter_vehicles.py documents how to build the data.
OpenAI Codex assisted with the GIS processing, code, layout and QA.
